New files this task will create: none.

INTENT
The Delivery page shows its daily chart and its card bars readably at the 7, 30 and 90 day windows.

Evidence, seen on a real dashboard at the 90 day window: the headline says 726 tasks landed, yet the Landed per day chart shows only zeros. The chart (snodo/web/static/delivery.js, snodo/web/static/delivery.css, classes delivery-chart, delivery-day, delivery-day-count, delivery-track, delivery-bar) gives every day a column of at least 2.5rem inside an overflow-x auto grid, so 90 days are far wider than the page; the browser opens it scrolled to the oldest days, which are empty, and the recent days with all the work are off screen. The per-day count label sits at the top of each column, far above a short bar, so a row of zeros floats over nothing. Separately, the text inside the bar segments of the Flow velocity and Flow distribution cards is meant to be invisible (the delivery-segment rule sets font-size 0) but the rule delivery-figure span sets a font size with higher specificity, so labels such as feature 80% show clipped under the bars and the weekly velocity bars show stray glyphs.

What it should do: the daily chart fits the width of the page for every window without sideways scrolling (narrow bars are fine at 90 days), shows the most recent days, puts a count only where it is not zero and close to its bar, and keeps the sparse date labels it has. The segment text in the card bars stays hidden, while the title attribute and aria labels keep carrying the numbers. The unit stays tasks landed per day; do not change the data or the endpoint. Read the existing tests for the static delivery page and its stylesheet and keep them meaningful: every delivery class keeps a style rule, hidden date labels take no space, styles use only local theme tokens.

CONSTRAINTS
Files that may change: snodo/web/static/delivery.css, snodo/web/static/delivery.js, tests/web/test_static_delivery.py, tests/web/test_static_delivery_style.py and CHANGELOG.md (one entry under [Unreleased] ending with the issue reference Fixes #<n>). No new state, status, severity or halt type.

ACCEPTANCE
At the 90 day window the daily chart fits without a horizontal scrollbar, and a test covers the zero-count and layout behaviour. A test or style check covers the segment text staying hidden. `make gate-task` is green.
