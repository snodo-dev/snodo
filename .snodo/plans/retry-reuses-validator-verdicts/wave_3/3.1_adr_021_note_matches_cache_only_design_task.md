# 3.1_adr_021_note_matches_cache_only_design

## INTENT
The amendment at the end of `docs/decisions/021-recovery-builds-from-original-task.md` ("Amendment — Retry verdict reuse (2026-11-07)", added by an earlier task of this plan) records a design that was then changed. It says a prior warn or blocker from a spec-only validator is judged again, and that an authorized warn "need not be dispatched again" because of its signed decision. Neither is what the code now does: since task 2.1 of this plan, a cached verdict of any severity (pass, warn or blocker) on an identical subject is answered from the verdict cache on a retry or recovery, and the signed decision of an authorized warn is applied afterwards by the policy (`packages/snodo-engine/src/snodo/engine/policy.py`, the DecisionRecord check around lines 193 to 211). The ADR should say what is true, because it is the record the next maintainer reads when they ask why `snodo run --retry` does not re-judge a validator that was just authorized.

Evidence for why the signed decision alone cannot be the mechanism: the DecisionRecord lookup (`find_adjudicated` in `packages/snodo-foundation/src/snodo/infrastructure/decisions.py`, around lines 260 to 304) binds signature, task lineage, validator id, severity and decision, but no spec or tree subject, so skipping dispatch on the strength of a decision could stand in for a verdict about a spec or tree that has since changed. The verdict cache keys on the subject and records that a result was reused, so it is the right place.

Keep the amendment short (one section, no new headings besides the existing one), keep the "Constraints preserved" section consistent with it, and keep the issue references that are still true.
