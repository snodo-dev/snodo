New files this task will create: docs/decisions/063-validator-fallback-on-operational-failure.md.

INTENT
Record the decision that validator models fail over only on operational failures, never on verdicts, so the rule has a home next to the records it builds on.

Evidence: the fallback list is merged and documented. The rule it follows is the same one validation already holds: a validator that produced no verdict is validator_error (ADR 015), a submitted verdict is final and an unusable one is not a reason to retry (ADR 059), and the llm section is closed to unknown keys (ADR 046).

What it should do: write ADR 063 in the style of the neighbouring records: Status, Context, Decision, Consequences, Alternatives. The decision: the validator model may be a short ordered list; the next model is tried only after an operational failure that survived the existing retries; no verdict, of any severity or unusable, ever triggers a switch; the classifier shares the list; each switch is one job-log line; an exhausted list is the existing validator_error. Name the alternative of switching on a disagreeing or low-quality verdict and say why it is rejected (a second opinion chosen after seeing the first is shopping for a verdict). Link the related records. Add the entry to the decisions index.

CONSTRAINTS
Files that may change: docs/decisions/063-validator-fallback-on-operational-failure.md, docs/decisions/README.md. Also edit CHANGELOG.md: add one entry under [Unreleased] ending "(Fixes #<n>)". Documentation only. Do not add any state, status, severity or halt type.
