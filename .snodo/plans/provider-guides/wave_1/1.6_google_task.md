INTENT
Write docs/providers/google.md, a step-by-step guide for the built-in Google (Gemini API) provider, ending at a passing `snodo models --check`.

PAGE SHAPE (shared by every provider guide)
1. What you need. 2. Store the key (default variable, `snodo config add`, `api_key_ref`, and that a literal or encrypted `api_key` wins over the environment variable). 3. Configure: the smallest `providers:` block, noting what the built-in catalog supplies. 4. Choose model ids: the form snodo maps to this provider, one example per role (coder, validator, classifier, recon), listing with `snodo models --provider google`. 5. Verify with `snodo models --check`. 6. Troubleshooting table.

THE CHAIN (confirm each point in code)
- The snodo provider name is `google`, while LiteLLM routes Gemini as "gemini/". Ids like "google/gemini/gemini-3.8-flash" and "gemini/gemini-3.8-flash" both reach this provider; check output shows `LiteLLM model: gemini/gemini-3.8-flash`. Read the exact mapping from the code.
- The default key variable comes from the built-in catalog.
- A recon run hit Gemini's per-minute input-token quota (HTTP 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED) on a paid tier; list it under troubleshooting as a quota limit on the Google side.
